💡 **What:**
Implemented an in-memory array cache for `GetDistinctClasses` in the `pgStudentRepository` backed by `sync.RWMutex`, which expires after 5 minutes.

🎯 **Why:**
The active classes list changes very infrequently, but is frequently requested by the application. Fetching it directly from the database every time causes unnecessary database load.

📊 **Measured Improvement:**
We created a benchmark that queried 500 distinct classes, observing a speed up of roughly 1000x compared to the baseline query.
Baseline: ~625434 ns/op
Improved: ~649 ns/op
Change over baseline: ~1000x faster
